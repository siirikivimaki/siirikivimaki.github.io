Welcome to my academic website. I am a mathematician working in the field of set theory. Currently I am a Charles B. Morrey Jr. Math Fellow (formerly Morrey Visiting Assistant Professor) at University of California, Berkeley.

<img src="assets/images/me.jpg" alt="Siiri Kivimäki" style="width:280px; float:right; margin-left:20px; margin-bottom:20px;">

My research interests in set theory include forcing and combinatorics of uncountable objects, universality problems and model theory of infinitary logics. I obtained my PhD in May 2026 from Université Paris Cité and University of Helsinki, under the supervision of Juliette Kennedy, Jouko Väänänen and Boban Veličković. 

For more details, see my <a href="assets/pdfs/cv.pdf" target="_blank">CV</a>. See my research [here](/research).
